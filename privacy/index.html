Privacy Policy

Meowditation  ·  Effective June 10, 2026

The short version: Meowditation lets you create an account to save and sync your progress. We collect only what's needed to run your account and sync your data — your email, your name, and the content you create in the app. We never sell your data, show you ads, or track you across other apps.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect and store the following:

  • Account information — your email address and name (display name), provided when you sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple.
  • A user identifier — a unique account ID used to associate your data with your account.
  • Your app content — meditation session history (duration, date, mood), in-app progress (level, coins, streaks, plant growth), your companion's name and customisation choices, CBT thought records you create, and your app preferences.

You may also choose to use the app as a guest (anonymous sign-in) without providing your email or name. In that case we store only an anonymous user identifier and the app content above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to provide the app's core functionality:

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To save your progress and sync it across your devices
  • To enable features such as meditation tracking, quests, the shop, plants, and CBT thought records

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or tracking, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to manage accounts and store your synced data. Your data is transmitted securely and stored on Google's servers on our behalf. Firebase's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy and Security policy.

4. Sign-In Providers

You can sign in using email and password, Google, or Apple. When you use Google or Apple sign-in, that provider shares your email and name with us so we can create your account. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's and Apple's privacy policies. If you use Sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email with Apple's Private Relay.

5. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, the app writes your meditation duration to Apple Health as a Mindful Session. This data:

  • Is written to the Apple Health app on your device only
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be revoked at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Meowditation

6. In-App Purchases

Meowditation is free to download. If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information.

7. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking frameworks, and we do not access your device's advertising identifier. NSPrivacyTracking is set to false in our privacy manifest.

8. Notifications

If you enable reminders, local notifications are scheduled on-device. No notification data leaves your device.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at prinza2501@gmail.com. We will delete your data from our systems promptly upon request.

10. Children's Privacy

Meowditation is rated for ages 9 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions or concerns? Email us at prinza2501@gmail.com.
