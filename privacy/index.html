Privacy Policy

Meditation Cat  ·  Effective June 1, 2025

The short version: Meditation Cat does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Meditation Cat is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Meditation session history (duration, date, mood)
  • App preferences and settings
  • In-app progress (level, coins, streaks, plant growth)
  • Your cat's name and customisation choices
  • CBT thought records you create

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, the app writes your meditation duration to Apple Health as a Mindful Session. This data:

  • Is written to the Apple Health app on your device only
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be revoked at any time in Settings → Privacy → Health → Meditation Cat

3. In-App Purchases

Coin purchases are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by Apple and can be restored at any time.

4. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. NSPrivacyTracking is set to false in our privacy manifest.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, local notifications are scheduled on-device. No notification data leaves your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Meditation Cat does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains in-app purchases and is intended for general audiences.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
